Software Engineer - Internal Tools
We build the tools the rest of the company runs on: admin consoles, tenant and
licence management, provisioning dashboards. You will own features end to end,
from a Figma mockup to production. Stack is React, Node.js, Express and MongoDB,
with REST APIs and JWT auth. Some of the work touches SCIM provisioning and SSO,
so an appetite for identity plumbing helps. We deploy with Docker. You should be
comfortable in a small team where everyone reviews everyone. Swift feedback
loops, and we go to production daily.
