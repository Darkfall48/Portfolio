Infrastructure Engineer

Who we are: a profitable, bootstrapped company that has been shipping for eleven
years. We are not a startup and we do not pretend to be one. We value people who
finish things, who write down what they learned, and who are comfortable being
the only person who understands a system for a while. Hybrid, three days in the
office, and we mean it.

What the job actually is: keeping the lights on for a hybrid estate that has
grown faster than its documentation. VMware ESXi and Proxmox clusters on one
side, AWS EC2 and S3 workloads on the other, and a fleet of Windows Server boxes
in between. You will inherit Active Directory, GPO, Exchange Online and AzureAD,
and you will be asked to make them boring. Certificates, including SAN
certificates, reverse proxy configuration, DNS, firewalls and VPN are all yours.

We expect fluent PowerShell and enough Python to automate the rest. Terraform
and Ansible are on the roadmap, so exposure to either would be welcome.
