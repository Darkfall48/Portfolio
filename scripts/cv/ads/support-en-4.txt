Escalation Engineer

About us: we are a Series B company of 120 people, backed by tier-one investors,
with an office in Tel Aviv and a fully remote engineering group. We believe in
ownership, in blameless postmortems, and in hiring people who care more about
the customer than about being right. Our values are curiosity, candour and
craft. We offer equity, a learning budget, and a genuinely flat structure.

The role: you sit between support and R&D and own the cases nobody else could
close. That means reproducing a customer environment locally, reading through
container logs, and turning a vague complaint into a filed defect an engineer
can act on. You will spend real time in Zendesk, Jira and Confluence.

Requirements: Linux, Windows Server, Docker, an understanding of SAML and OIDC,
LDAP and Active Directory, TLS certificate debugging, and remote access over
RDP or SSH. REST APIs and Postman for reproducing calls. Python or Bash.
