Backend Engineer - Platform
You will work on the services behind our product: a REST API in Node.js and
Express, MongoDB for most of the data and MySQL for the parts that never got
migrated, and a provisioning layer that speaks SCIM and SAML to our customers'
identity providers. JWT everywhere. Everything runs in Docker on AWS, with
GitHub Actions for CI and Grafana and Prometheus for monitoring. You will write
Python for the tooling around it. We care about tests and about not being woken
up at night. Experience with Golang or PostgreSQL would be an advantage.
