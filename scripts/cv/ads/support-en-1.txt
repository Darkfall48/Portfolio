Senior Technical Support Engineer (Tier 3)
We are a cybersecurity vendor shipping a Zero Trust access product on-premise,
in the cloud and in DMZ environments. You will own escalated cases end to end
and work with cross-functional teams to go the extra mile for our customers,
delivering swift resolution on the toughest issues.
Requirements: strong troubleshooting on Linux and Windows Server, hands-on with
Docker and docker-compose, solid grasp of SAML, OIDC, SCIM and LDAP, TLS
certificate debugging, and remote access protocols such as RDP and SSH.
Experience with Zendesk and Jira, and writing customer-facing documentation in
Confluence. Kubernetes and Datadog exposure a plus. Scripting in Python or Bash.
