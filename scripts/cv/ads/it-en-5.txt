Network and Systems Administrator
A manufacturing group with six sites is hiring a network administrator to take
over its internal infrastructure. You will manage the Fortinet estate, site to
site VPN tunnels, DNS zones and firewall rules, and the reverse proxy in front
of our internal applications. On the systems side you will run Windows Server,
Active Directory and GPO, VMware hosts and a set of multi-OS virtual machines,
and you will administer Microsoft 365 and Exchange for 600 mailboxes. Device
management goes through Intune. Scripting is expected, in PowerShell above all.
Certificate lifecycle, including SAN certificates, sits with this role. You
report to the head of IT and you go on call one week in four.
